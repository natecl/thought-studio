# Glossary

## Agent

An AI Participant with an identity, role, permissions, tools, spatial scope, and attributed activity.

## Board

The primary shared spatial environment. It contains material, relationships, participants, and history.

## Connection

A visual or optionally semantic relationship between board entities.

## Creation

A deliberate output formed from board material while retaining lineage to its sources and contributors.

## Frame

A bounded Board region with a purpose, such as a question, system, work area, presentation step, agent assignment, or Creation boundary.

## Lens

A non-destructive way to view or interpret Board state, such as chronology, provenance, uncertainty, or presentation order.

## Mark

Direct, low-structure expression such as handwriting, ink, a highlight, or a rough drawing.

## Material

The broad category for anything that can participate in thought on the Board, including Marks, Objects, Sources, and imported content.

## Object

An identifiable and placeable Board unit, such as text, a note, shape, image, embed, file, or group.

## Participant

A person or Agent present on a Board.

## Provenance

Information describing the origin, authorship, derivation, and relevant history of Board material.

## Source

External or imported material with provenance, such as a video, document, webpage, image, conversation, dataset, or code artifact.

## Spatial authorship

The meaning a user creates through position, distance, grouping, containment, scale, and connection on the Board.

## Thought Studio

The product and environment in which humans, information, and AI agents think together spatially.
