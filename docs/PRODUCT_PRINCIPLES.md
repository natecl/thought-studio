# Product principles

These principles are evaluation criteria. A feature that conflicts with them requires an explicit decision record.

## 1. The board is the product

The board is the primary environment for thought, collaboration, sources, agents, and creation. New capabilities should deepen the board rather than create competing centers of gravity.

## 2. Blankness is sacred

The default board begins empty and immediately usable. Templates, prompts, and agent suggestions are available by choice, not imposed before the user has acted.

## 3. Structure emerges after thought

People should not need to choose a schema, object type, folder, or workflow before placing an idea. Informal material can become structured later without being recreated.

## 4. Spatial arrangement carries meaning

Position, distance, grouping, scale, containment, sequence, and connection are part of the user's thought. Automation must preserve spatial authorship unless the user explicitly requests a transformation.

## 5. Ink and direct manipulation come first

Writing, drawing, placing, moving, circling, connecting, and erasing should feel immediate. The intelligence of the product should appear after or alongside expression, not obstruct it.

## 6. AI participates visibly

Agents are participants on the board, not hidden processes behind an answer box. Their scope, activity, sources, changes, and identity must be legible. Meaningful changes must be reversible.

## 7. Everything can become material

Documents, videos, webpages, images, conversations, data, code, and other boards should be able to enter the spatial context with useful fidelity and provenance.

## 8. Integrations serve the board

Connected applications provide material, references, or destinations. They should not introduce separate interaction systems that fragment the core experience.

## 9. Creations retain lineage

A finished output should remain connected to the frames, objects, people, agents, and sources that produced it. Traceability should be useful without making the interface feel forensic.

## 10. Multiplayer means shared understanding

Collaboration is not merely simultaneous cursors. Participants should be able to enter, interpret, challenge, extend, and replay the same evolving model.

## 11. Beauty is functional

Visual calm, tactile feedback, motion, typography, color, spacing, and performance shape the quality of thought. Beauty and utility are coequal acceptance criteria.

## 12. Complexity should unfold gradually

The blank board is simple. Semantic connections, agent controls, permissions, history, lenses, and advanced systems reveal themselves as the user needs them.

## 13. The user remains an author

The product can accelerate research, synthesis, organization, and composition, but it must preserve the user's judgment, voice, and ability to understand the result.

## Product review questions

Before accepting a feature, ask:

- Does it make the board more powerful or distract from it?
- Does it preserve the ability to begin without structure?
- Does it respect spatial meaning?
- Can people understand what an agent did?
- Does it preserve provenance and reversibility?
- Does it feel native to the same set of primitives?
- Is the interaction beautiful enough to invite repeated thought?
