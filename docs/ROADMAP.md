# Initial roadmap

This roadmap orders learning, not just feature delivery. Dates are intentionally omitted until the founding product decisions are made.

## Stage 0: Concept alignment

**Goal:** Make the founding thesis coherent and testable.

- Maintain product context, vision, principles, PRD, and object model.
- Select the first audience and creation outcome.
- Interview people whose work moves from ambiguity to a system or creation.
- Collect examples of physical whiteboard sessions and fragmented digital workflows.
- Define the signature demonstration.

**Exit signal:** The team can explain one initial user, one painful workflow, and one complete Board-based outcome without describing an everything app.

## Stage 1: Interaction prototype

**Goal:** Prove that digital whiteboarding can feel immediate, beautiful, and structurally generative.

- Blank-board entry
- Text, ink, notes, images, and simple URL Sources
- Pan, zoom, selection, grouping, Connections, and Frames
- Visual exploration of calm interface, motion, and tactile feedback
- Frame-to-Creation concept

This may begin as a high-fidelity prototype before durable backend architecture.

**Exit signal:** Target users can move from blankness to a meaningful framed system without instruction.

## Stage 2: Agent-native prototype

**Goal:** Prove that an agent can participate spatially without replacing or obscuring human thought.

- Lasso-and-ask
- Bounded agent work area
- Visible identity and activity
- Propose, accept, reject, and revert
- Source-backed contributions
- Frame-to-structured-text Creation

**Exit signal:** Users prefer at least one spatial agent workflow to performing the same work in a detached chat.

## Stage 3: Human multiplayer

**Goal:** Prove that the Board supports shared understanding.

- Presence, cursors, and selections
- Attributed contributions
- Real-time conflict handling
- Asynchronous activity and walkthrough
- Initial sharing and permissions

**Exit signal:** A collaborator can enter an existing Board, understand its system, and extend it without a separate context briefing.

## Stage 4: Connected material

**Goal:** Prove the value of using the Board as a unified context surface.

- Rich YouTube references
- Notion documents
- Webpages and PDFs
- Additional integrations chosen from observed demand
- Permission-aware provenance

**Exit signal:** Connected Sources materially reduce context reconstruction and are reused in thought or Creations.

## Stage 5: Multiplayer agents and durable systems

**Goal:** Extend from single-agent assistance to legible teams of humans and agents.

- Named agent roles
- Frame assignments
- Visible delegation
- Lenses for source, participant, history, and uncertainty
- Reusable systems and cross-Board relationships

**Exit signal:** Multiple agents improve a complex session without reducing human comprehension or control.

## Immediate decisions

1. Choose the first target user.
2. Choose the first Creation outcome.
3. Choose the primary initial input surface: desktop pointer/keyboard, tablet pen/touch, or responsive support for both.
4. Define the first five-minute demo.
5. Prototype the visual language before selecting deep infrastructure.
